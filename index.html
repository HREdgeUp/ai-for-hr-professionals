    01 Introduction: Why AI, Why Now for HR?

    Artificial intelligence is reshaping how people leaders recruit, onboard, develop, retain, and support employees. The question is no longer whether to use AI, but how to use it responsibly and effectively.

    This guide is designed for People Leaders who are curious, thoughtful, and ready to lead their organizations into this moment with intention. You do not need a technical background to use AI well — you need strong human judgment, empathy, and a commitment to doing right by your people.

    The Core Principle

    AI amplifies human capability. It does not replace human responsibility.

    What AI Can Do

    • Draft job descriptions and policy documents
    • Analyze engagement survey data
    • Generate onboarding plans
    • Create performance frameworks
    • Summarize meetings
    • Build interview guides
    • Research compensation trends
    • Produce employee communications

    What AI Cannot Replace

    • Human judgment
    • Authententic relationships
    • Ethical decision‑making
    • Contextual understanding
    • Leadership accountability

    02 The CLEAR Framework

    C — Context

    Set the scene.

    L — Language

    Specify tone.

    E — Examples

    Show what good looks like.

    A — Ask

    State your request clearly.

    R — Refine

    Iterate to excellence.

    Example CLEAR prompt:

    “Context: I’m an HR Director supporting a 200‑person engineering org going through a reorg.
    Language: Professional, clear, empathetic.
    Examples: Here are two past messages that landed well.
    Ask: Draft a manager‑ready announcement and a separate employee‑ready announcement.
    Refine: Shorten both versions to under 300 words and add 3 bullet points of key actions.”

    04 Human‑Centered Guardrails

    • 1. Never Input Personal Data — Protect privacy and confidentiality.
    • 2. Check for Bias — Review outputs for fairness and unintended patterns.
    • 3. Maintain Human Connection — AI supports; it does not replace human presence.
    • 4. Know the Legal Landscape — Regulations are evolving; stay aligned with counsel.
    • 5. Build an Audit Trail — Document when and how AI was used in decisions.
