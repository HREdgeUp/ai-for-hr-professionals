04 Human-Centered Guardrails

  • Never input personal employee data.
  • Actively check for bias. Review outputs for gendered language or discriminatory patterns.
  • Maintain the human relationship. AI should never replace direct human conversations.
  • Know your legal landscape. Understand regulations before using AI in employment decisions.
  • Build an audit trail. Document prompts, tools, outputs, and human review.

Talent Acquisition | Job Description

"Act as a senior HR professional. I am hiring a [job title] at a [company size] [industry] company.
Our culture values [two to three values]. Write an inclusive, compelling job description in plain
language, avoiding jargon. Include: a role summary, key responsibilities in six to eight bullets,
qualifications separated into must-have and nice-to-have, and a culture and benefits statement.
Keep it under 500 words."
